Week 929
========

Hello! 👋

These are a bit late and cover the past two weeks so I'm going to keep them brief.

- We were lower on the ground during week 928 due to half term but were back to full strength in week 929.

- Most of our time was spent working on [Mavis](https://www.manage-vaccinations-in-schools.nhs.uk/start) as is the norm.

- I enjoyed pairing/mobbing on a tricky problem with [Chris](/chris-lowis) and [James](/james-mead) for a day or so. We've yet again fallen out of the habit of doing this and so it was a good reminder of how much I like it.

- Our migration from 1Password to [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) is now complete. As far as I can tell Bitwarden is at feature parity to 1Password (for at least the things I need) but it doesn't feel quite as polished.

- I've updated my desktop machine to Ubuntu 26.04 LTS. I took the opportunity to reinstall from scratch and enable full disk encryption at the same time.

- We've continued to have a number of bizdev conversations. Thanks again to Chris for leading on these.

- We've switched our group income protection and critical illness insurance to Zurich as recommended by [Drewberry](https://www.drewberryinsurance.co.uk/).

- We're really pleased to see that the work we helped with to get Scratch into the RPF Code Editor is [now live](https://www.raspberrypi.org/blog/reintroducing-code-classroom-now-with-the-blocks-editor/)! Congratulations to everyone involved.

- We happily increased our [Solidfund](https://www.solidfund.coop/) contributions to support their great work.

Until next time.

-- Chris
