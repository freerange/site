Week 932
========

Greetings! 

It's really cold, isn't it? I've been considering buying a heated blanket. I'm currently making-do with a scarf and an unusually-timed (it's 445pm!!) cup-a-soup. 

But you didn't come here for weather tips did you? No. You came here for insight. 

Oh. 

Chris, James and I got together on Thursday for our quarterly-ish in-person meeting. We strolled around Cambridge a little bit, but mostly warmed ourselves with all-day breakfasts at the [Old Bicycle Shop](https://www.oldbicycleshop.com/) and log fires at [The Mill](https://www.themillpubcambridge.com/). 

We've been working hard on our current project and have had less time together than usual. It was good to clear our "things to discuss" backlog in Trello and make plans for 2027. In the latter category we're thinking of working with a business coach/advisor to get some outside perspective on our plans for world domination/bankruptcy. 

Meanwhile in Mavis-land we've been making progress on supporting new formats for school-provided data imports and improving the modelling of parental consents. We've also been helping with less concrete team and process improvements. Chris did some great work taming a very noisy Sentry alert channel and making it much more actionable and useful. I spent some time helping the team plan some upcoming work and "story map" the most important features. I think this kind of work is very valuable but the impact of it is harder to measure - it's about doing less to achieve more. 

Elsewhere I've been feeling nostalgic about the old web. But! I really enjoyed [this presentation](https://dn711504.ca.archive.org/0/items/small_web/11787-48384.mp4) about just how big the "small web" is in 2026. I spent a good hour clicking around [Rek Bell's](https://kokorobot.ca/) beautifully illustrated site and side-projects, and even explored [Gemini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(protocol)) (not that one) using the [Lagrange](https://gmi.skyjake.fi/lagrange/) browser. Nostalgia, it seems, [is not what it used to be](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2013/07/06/nostalgia-is-not/).

Stay warm! 

-- Chris
